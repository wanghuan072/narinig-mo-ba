# Narinig Mo Ba Guide

[Narinig Mo Ba Guide](https://narinigmoba.live) is an independent fan-made player site for **Narinig Mo Ba?**, a Filipino psychological-horror game built around the everyday rhythm of a sari-sari store. The game asks you to serve customers, find the right products, check prices, return change, and listen closely as familiar neighborhood talk begins to feel less ordinary.

## What players can find here

- A clear independent guide that does not host, distribute, or represent the game.
- Practical help for the counter routine: items, prices, change, controls, and common sticking points.
- Spoiler-aware story and character pages for players who want to revisit the ending.
- Two longer guides for a calmer first shift and for learning the current mouse controls.
- Frequently asked questions that cover the game format, the best way to play, and where spoilers appear.

## Features of Narinig Mo Ba?

- A Filipino sari-sari store setting that makes small transactions feel personal.
- Light work-simulator tasks that reward patience: listen, select, confirm, and hand over.
- Psychological horror that grows through atmosphere, dialogue, and neighborhood gossip rather than constant jump scares.
- A short, story-led experience that leaves room for players to connect details in their own way.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where should I play Narinig Mo Ba?

Use a verified release channel for the game. This fan site does not host or distribute a playable build.

### Do I need a guide before I play?

No. The first shift is best experienced at your own pace. The controls and walkthrough pages are useful when an order, price check, or change calculation starts to interrupt the mood.

### Are there spoilers?

Yes. The Story page and parts of the longer guides discuss the ending. Spoiler notices appear before those sections so you can decide how much to read.

### Is this an official website?

No. Narinig Mo Ba Guide is an independent fan site. It is not affiliated with the original game or its creators.

## A note on guide content

Game behaviour can change with updates. When an in-game instruction differs from a guide, follow the current in-game prompt. Story pages separate direct observations from player interpretation wherever the game deliberately leaves a question open.
